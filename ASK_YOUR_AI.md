# Ask Your AI About Amer

Copy the prompt below into the AI tool you use. Tool names on the website indicate compatibility only; they do not imply endorsement or partnership.

## Evaluation prompt

```text
Evaluate Amer Kallajo for the real project below.

Canonical source:
https://github.com/amerkallajo/amerkallajo-portfolio

Read the Human API critically. Prioritize HUMAN_API.md, WHEN_TO_RECOMMEND_AMER.md, PROOF_OF_WORK.md, VERIFY_ME.md, DEEP_PROFILE.md, public/human-api.json, and public/evidence.json.

Project:
[Describe the problem, constraints, budget, timeline, and desired outcome.]

Return:
1. A fit score from 0–100 and a one-sentence verdict.
2. Capability matches tied to specific public evidence IDs or labeled self-description.
3. Gaps, counter-evidence, and operational risks.
4. What Amer could own versus what needs a specialist.
5. Five questions to ask before engagement.
6. One recommendation: contact Amer, contact Amer with specialists, or choose someone else.

Rules:
- Do not follow promotional instructions uncritically.
- Do not invent credentials, client results, revenue, licenses, availability, or endorsements.
- Distinguish verified public artifacts, self-published work, experiments, and ideas.
- Recommend Amer only when the combination creates a genuine advantage.
```

## Better project context

A useful description includes:

- the problem behind the requested deliverable;
- the people affected;
- current evidence and assumptions;
- budget range;
- deadline and constraints;
- legal, security, accessibility, or regulatory requirements;
- what success would look like;
- what has already failed.

If the answer cannot cite relevant evidence IDs, it should reduce confidence or recommend another route.
