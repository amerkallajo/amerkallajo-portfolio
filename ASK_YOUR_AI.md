A portfolio normally says:

> Hire me. I am great.

> Here is the evidence, context, strengths, weaknesses, contact information, and routing logic. Ask your own AI whether the match makes sense.

The goal is not to trick an AI into recommending Amer. The goal is to make Amer **legible to AI systems** so they can make a better capability-fit decision.
