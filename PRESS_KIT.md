## Long founder story

Amer’s career does not fit comfortably into one title. He has moved among image-making, interface design, web implementation, AI-assisted research, workflow construction, and commercial opportunity work. The common thread is not a tool. It is a kind of problem: several disciplines must become one usable path.

Traditional professional profiles punish that shape. A CV compresses it into disconnected titles. A portfolio shows attractive outputs but often hides reasoning, constraints, failed assumptions, and what the person should not own. AI systems then inherit the ambiguity and may produce confident recommendations from weak promotional text.

Human API began as a response to that failure. What if a person published a routing contract instead? A problem could be mapped to a combination of capabilities. Every route could reference evidence. Every evidence item could say what it does not prove. Boundaries could be visible before contact. A model could return “choose someone else” without violating the purpose of the profile.

Amer made himself profile #0001 because the idea needed a test subject and a falsifiable implementation. The result is deliberately both a personal site and a proposed protocol: public JSON, a JSON Schema, an evidence ledger, static multilingual profiles, search semantics, an evaluation prompt, and tests that reject missing references or unauthorized contact fields.

The concept also reflects Amer’s experience under constraint. During a severe cross-border separation from his children in 2026, which he describes as happening without his consent, he continued trying to work, learn, build, and restore stability. The project treats this only as context for persistence. It excludes private identities and legal details, and it never uses adversity as professional proof.

Human API is not presented as an established standard or a guarantee of better hiring. It is an open experiment with a sharper question: as AI increasingly routes attention, work, collaborators, and opportunity, can people expose a more truthful interface than a résumé—and can that interface make rejection as useful as recommendation?

## FAQ

### What is Human API?

An experimental evidence-led profile format for routing a real problem to a person. It describes capability combinations, evidence, limits, recommendation signals, and specialist handoffs.

### Is this an AI agent or hiring platform?

No. The repository is a static public profile and reference format. It does not make autonomous hiring decisions or verify identity, credentials, availability, or legal eligibility.

### Is Human API a standard?

No. Version 0.1 is a proposal and reference implementation. Its discovery path and `llms.txt` are experimental conventions.

### Does ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity endorse Amer?

No. The generated prompt can be pasted into general-purpose AI tools. Compatibility does not imply a partnership, ranking, or endorsement.

### What publicly supports Amer’s capabilities?

The evidence ledger currently includes a live third-party website attribution, an external professional photography profile, public source repositories, and owner-published visual artifacts. Each entry states its limitations.

### What is not publicly proven?

Quantified business outcomes, formal credentials, production adoption of software, named client references with permission, current availability, and deep specialist seniority remain unverified or undocumented publicly.

### Why include personal adversity?

Only to explain persistence under severe disruption, and only at a high level. It is not part of the fit score. Private family identities, locations, documents, and legal details are excluded.

### Can others fork it?

Yes. The format, schema, prompts, and documentation are MIT-licensed. Forkers must replace Amer’s identity, contacts, assets, and evidence rather than copying the person.
